Who am I?

I'm an AI engineer in training at IIT Jodhpur, where I'm pursuing an M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence after clearing GATE CSE 2025. Before that, I earned my B.Tech in Computer Science from Rashtriya Raksha University, Gandhinagar.

My research is on bias in federated learning: how to train a shared model across hospitals or devices when each one sees a skewed slice of the data, without ever pooling that data. I'm applying it to Monkeypox detection from skin images.

Outside research, I like the engineering side of ML. I've built an MLOps pipeline that retrains, serves and monitors a model on its own, and a semantic search engine over a million records that answers in under 30 ms. I started out writing distributed Java at an eDiscovery company, and I still enjoy making things scale.
